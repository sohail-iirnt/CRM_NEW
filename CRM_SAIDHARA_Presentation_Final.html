08 · PDI QUALITY

PDI is the
quality gate.

Inspection record

PDI selects an Admin-managed inspector and records result, total checked, defective/damaged quantity, approved quantity and remarks.

InspectorPDI ResultTotal CheckedDefectiveApproved

Result drives the next step

PDI OK lets the ticket continue to Dispatch. ALL MATERIAL DAMAGE can start a replacement-batch cycle. NO STOCK is used only after Warehouse reports no stock.

Inspector directory: Admin manages inspectors; PDI only selects from the live directory.
09 · BATCH REINSPECTION

Assign.
Reassign. Reinspect.

Replacement batches remain attached to the same ticket. The PDI quantity is based on the quantity actually allocated by Warehouse for that batch.

01
Batch #1Warehouse allocates a quantity; PDI inspects that exact batch quantity.
02
All damagedIf checked quantity equals defective quantity and ALL MATERIAL DAMAGE is selected, replacement request becomes available.
03
Batch #2…NWarehouse allocates the next available quantity; PDI sees the new batch and its exact quantity.
04
Finish / exhaustPDI completes a usable batch or the Warehouse reports no more stock.

Example: 3 allocated

PDI sees Batch #1 / Qty 3. If 1 is defective and 2 are OK, PDI can complete inspection and the ticket continues normally.

Example: 3 of 3 damaged

PDI records 3 defective, requests a replacement and Warehouse can allocate Batch #2 or report no stock.

18 · CRM CLOSED TICKETS

Closure has
two stories.

Closed by Logistics

Normal closure after Logistics completes ETA and Actual Branch Reporting requirements. CRM identifies these as Logistics closures.

Closed due to No Stock

Warehouse reports no stock, PDI confirms NO STOCK and the ticket closes through the no-stock path.

Reassigned to OEC Team

For no-stock closed tickets, CRM can mark Reassigned to OEC Team, recording the handoff for the separate OEC system while the CRM ticket remains closed.
