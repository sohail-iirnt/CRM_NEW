01 · Presentation Overview

What is
SAIDHARA?

A single portal where every department works around the same ticket, follows a defined process and leaves a traceable operational history.

One Ticket

The ticket remains the common operational record from CRM creation through final closure.

One Workflow

Ownership moves from CRM to Warehouse, PDI, Dispatch and Logistics in a controlled sequence.

One View

Status, history, timestamps, activity and analytics make progress visible to the right teams.

02 · Operating Model

Five teams.
One operation.

CRMCreate & coordinate
WAREHOUSEMove & dispatch
PDIInspect & decide

The operating principle
Each team completes its responsibility, records the required information and hands the ticket forward without losing its history.
03 · CRM Team

CRM
starts it.

CRM creates the business record and begins the operational journey.

CRM responsibilities

  • Create inbound and outbound tickets.
  • Capture customer, item code, quantity, shed and destination.
  • Monitor current ticket status.
  • Import tickets using CSV.
  • Export the complete ticket dataset.

Handoff to operations

CreateValidateMonitorCoordinate

Once the ticket is ready, Warehouse receives the operational work while CRM retains visibility of the journey.

04 · Warehouse Team

Warehouse
moves it.

Warehouse owns physical material movement, shed operations and dispatch execution.

Movement

  • Receive operational tickets.
  • Work by shed.
  • Manage material movement and quantities.
  • Support batch reinspection.

Dedicated dispatch

For inbound and outbound dedicated dispatch:

Vehicle NumberFO Number
Load Number → Logistics
Warehouse does not collect Load Number.

Courier dispatch

Courier movement uses:

Courier NameTracking ID

Dispatch action and timestamp remain part of the ticket history.

05 · PDI Team

PDI
decides.

PDI validates material condition and records the inspection outcome.

Inspection responsibilities

  • Select an Admin-created inspector.
  • Record total checked quantity.
  • Record damaged / defective quantity.
  • Add remarks and inspection result.
  • Complete pass/fail workflow.

Inspector ownership

PDI selects from the central Admin-managed inspector directory.

No PDI-side creationLive directoryAdmin managed

When Admin adds an inspector, the PDI directory can update without duplicating inspector records.
