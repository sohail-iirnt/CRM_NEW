01 · Introduction

Why CRM SAIDHARA?

The portal connects the complete operational chain so teams can work from the same ticket, understand current ownership and trace what happened before.

One source of truth

One ticket carries business, operational, status and historical information from creation to closure.

Clear ownership

Each team has a defined responsibility and receives the information required for its stage.

Operational visibility

Live status, history, audit events, search and analytics reduce manual status chasing.

What CRM owns

  • Create inbound and outbound tickets.
  • Capture customer, item, quantity, destination and shed.
  • Monitor current status and operational progress.
  • Import tickets through CSV.
