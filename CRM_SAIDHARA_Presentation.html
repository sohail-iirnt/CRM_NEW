03 · CRM

CRM starts
the journey.

CRM captures the business requirement and creates the operational ticket that becomes the single source of truth for every downstream team.

CRM responsibilities

  • Create inbound and outbound tickets.
  • Capture customer, item, quantity, destination and shed information.
  • Track current ticket status and operational ownership.
  • Import tickets in bulk through CSV.
  • Export the complete ticket dataset with current status.

Ticket enters operations

Ticket Created→Validated→Warehouse

From this point, the ticket follows its operational workflow while preserving its history and identity.

04 · Warehouse

Material moves
with control.

Warehouse manages physical material movement, shed operations and dispatch. The responsibility boundaries are intentionally clear.

Movement

  • Receive operational tickets.
  • Work by shed.
  • Manage quantities and movement.
  • Prepare material for dispatch.

Dedicated Dispatch

Warehouse records:

Vehicle NumberFO Number

InboundOutbound

Load Number is not entered here.

Courier Dispatch

For courier movement, Warehouse records:

Courier NameTracking ID

Dispatch activity and timestamps remain available in the ticket history.

05 · PDI

Inspection becomes
a decision.

PDI validates material condition and records the quality outcome. Inspectors are maintained by Admin and selected from the live directory.

Inspection

  • Select an Admin-created inspector.
  • Record total checked quantity.
  • Record defective / damaged quantity.
  • Enter remarks and inspection result.
  • Continue normal pass/fail workflow.

Reinspection loop

PDI→Damaged→Request Batch→Warehouse→PDI

If Warehouse has no remaining stock, it can report No More Stock Available and PDI can close the case as All Material Damaged — No Stock.

06 · Logistics

Shipment moves
toward closure.

Logistics owns the transport information after dispatch: Load Number, ETA, transit progression and branch reporting.

Load & ETA

Logistics receives the dispatched ticket and records:

Load NumberETA

Both values are persisted and included in the operational audit trail.

Transit

After logistics processing, the ticket progresses to:

In Transit→Branch Reporting

Each major transition gets a timestamp.

Closure

The final operational information includes the actual branch reporting date and closure event.

Reported→Closed
