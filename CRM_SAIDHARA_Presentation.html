02 · Operating Model

Five teams.
One connected operation.

CRM

Create · validate · coordinate · monitor

Warehouse

Move · shed · dispatch · reinspection

PDI

Inspect · decide · damage · reinspect

Logistics

Load · ETA · transit · reporting

Admin

Users · access · inspectors · governance

Operating principle: each team completes its responsibility, records the required information and hands the ticket forward without losing its history.
03 · CRM Team

CRM
starts it.

CRM creates the business record and begins the operational journey.

CRM responsibilities

  • Create inbound and outbound tickets.
  • Capture customer, item code, quantity, shed and destination.
  • Track current status and ownership.
  • Import tickets through CSV.
  • Export the complete ticket dataset.

Handoff

Ticket IDCustomerItemShedQuantityDestination

Once the ticket is ready, Warehouse receives the operational work while CRM retains visibility.

05 · PDI Team

PDI
decides quality.

PDI validates material condition. Inspectors are centrally managed by Admin and selected from the live directory.

Inspection

  • Select Admin-created inspector.
  • Record total checked.
  • Record defective / damaged quantity.
  • Record remarks and result.

Important boundary

PDI does not create inspectors.

Admin → Inspector Directory → PDI
New inspectors become available through the Firebase-backed live directory.
06 · PDI Reinspection

When material
is damaged.

The inspection process can loop safely through batch replacement until the material passes or Warehouse confirms no stock remains.

PDI detects damage→Request new batch→Warehouse provides batch→PDI reinspects
No stock remaining: Warehouse reports No More Stock Available → PDI can record All Material Damaged — No Stock.
