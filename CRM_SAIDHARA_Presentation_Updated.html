05 · PDI QUALITY CONTROL

Inspection becomes
a decision engine.

PDI records the inspector, result, total checked, defective quantity and remarks. The next action depends on the inspection outcome.

PDI OK / PARTIAL DEFECTPROCEED

When checked material has approved quantity, the completed batch is recorded and the ticket continues toward dispatch. Example: 3 checked, 1 defective → 2 approved → ticket continues to Ready for Dispatch.

PDI Inspection→Batch Completed→Approved Qty→Ready for Dispatch
ALL MATERIAL DAMAGEREINSPECTION

When all checked material is defective, the batch is marked defective and a new-batch request becomes available for Warehouse on the same ticket.

All Damage→Defective Batch→New Batch Requested→Warehouse
WAREHOUSE · NO STOCKCLOSURE

Warehouse can explicitly report that no replacement stock is available. PDI sees the warehouse update, confirms the no-stock outcome, and the ticket closes with a clear no-stock reason.

Warehouse→No Stock→PDI Confirm→Closed
BATCH HISTORYTRACEABLE

Each replacement batch can remain visible with batch number, quantity, allocation timing, completion state and no-stock remarks where applicable.

Batch #1→Batch #2→Batch #3→Final Decision
06 · WAREHOUSE & DISPATCH

Physical work is
captured before handoff.

Warehouse separates movement, replacement-batch response and dispatch. Dispatch completion hands the ticket to Logistics with dedicated or courier information.

WAREHOUSE QUEUES

Operational workspace

  • Pending Movement queue with shed filtering.
  • PDI reinspection requests for new batch or no-stock response.
  • Ready for Dispatch queue.
  • Warehouse History & Audit.

Warehouse → Logistics handoff

Dedicated dispatch records vehicle number, load number and freight order number. Courier dispatch records courier name and tracking ID. The dispatch timestamp is preserved with the ticket.

07 · LOGISTICS

From dispatch
to actual reporting.

03 · REPORT & CLOSE

09 · TICKET DETAIL & INTELLIGENCE

The ticket detail window
explains the whole story.

The updated Ticket Detail experience brings current state, timestamps, PDI/batch operations, dispatch, logistics and activity history into one readable operational view.

WORKFLOW MONITOR

Important timestamps

Ticket Created, Warehousing Movement, Received for PDI, Inspection Done, New Batch Provided, No More Stock Available, Dispatch Done, Logistics Updated, In Transit, Ticket Closed and PDI no-stock closure can be represented as milestones.

PDI / BATCH

Readable operational data

Latest batch, quantity, completion time, defective and approved quantities, inspector, result, request count, remarks and batch history are presented as structured information.

NO-STOCK CLOSURE

Clear exception handling

No-stock tickets are visibly identified so a closed ticket is not confused with a normal logistics closure. Warehouse remarks and closure reason remain part of the story.

ACTIVITY TIMELINE

Who did what, when

Operational actions are presented with human-readable titles, timestamps, descriptions and status transitions instead of raw action codes.

CRM EXPORTS

Portable reporting

Ticket CSV output includes current workflow status plus ETA and Actual Branch Reporting Date for logistics tracking. PDF export remains available for operational sharing.

PDI HISTORY

Inspection evidence

Department history can filter PDI activity and completed inspections can be represented through printable PDI slips for operational documentation.
