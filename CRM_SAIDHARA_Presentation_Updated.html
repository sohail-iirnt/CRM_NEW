06 · PDI Team

PDI
decides quality.

PDI validates material condition. Inspectors are centrally managed by Admin and selected from the live directory.

Inspection

  • Select Admin-created inspector.
  • Record total checked.
  • Record defective / damaged quantity.
  • Record remarks and result.

Control boundary

PDI does not create inspectors.

Admin → Inspector Directory → PDI
New inspectors become available through the Firebase-backed live directory.
07 · PDI Reinspection

When material
is damaged.

The inspection process can loop safely through batch replacement until the material passes or Warehouse confirms no stock remains.

PDI detects damage→Request new batch→Warehouse provides batch→PDI reinspects
All material damaged: the current batch becomes defective and the ticket moves back through the Warehouse replacement workflow.
08 · No-Stock Branch

The safe stop
when stock ends.

PDI requests replacement

Material is fully defective and another batch is required.

→

Warehouse checks stock

If no replacement stock remains, Warehouse records No Stock and sends the update back to PDI.

NO STOCK BRANCH

PDI sees the Warehouse update, confirms the no-stock outcome, records NO STOCK, completes inspection and closes the ticket with a dedicated no-stock closure reason.

12 · Live Journey — Reinspection Path

Damage does not
break the journey.

02 · New batch requested

13 · Live Journey — No Stock

When Warehouse says
“nothing left”.

Warehouse update

Warehouse marks No Stock Available and records remarks. The ticket is returned to PDI with that operational signal.

NO STOCK · WAREHOUSE

PDI confirmation

PDI sees the no-stock update, confirms the outcome, records NO STOCK and completes the inspection.

NO STOCK · CLOSED
Closure is explicit: the no-stock closure carries its own reason and remains visible in Ticket Detail and operational history.
